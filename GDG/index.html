Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining and flourishing at GDG on Campus ITER.

Is membership in GDG on Campus ITER free? expand_more

Yes, 100% free! All SOA ITER students from any branch, academic year, or background can join our community without any membership fees or registration charges.

Do I need previous coding experience to join? expand_more

Not at all. We welcome absolute beginners, hobbyists, and advanced engineers alike. Our curriculum ranges from zero-prerequisite bootcamps in Web and Android to advanced multimodal AI agent hackathons.

What Google technologies and developer tracks will I explore? expand_more

You will gain practical experience with Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Gemini 1.5 API, Android Jetpack Compose, Flutter, Firebase, TensorFlow, and open-source Docker/Kubernetes workflows.

How can I earn official Google skill badges and certificates? expand_more

By participating in Google Cloud Study Jams, completing Qwiklabs quests, and submitting real software builds to the global annual Google Solution Challenge. Verified credentials are digitally verifiable on LinkedIn.

Where do regular event updates and workshop invites get posted? expand_more

All immediate updates, RSVP links, and mentor hours are broadcast on our official WhatsApp Community and our Google Developer Chapter platform on gdgociter.in.
